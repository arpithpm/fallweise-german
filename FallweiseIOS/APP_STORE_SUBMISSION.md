## Description

Fallweise makes German feel approachable through short, structured lessons that combine understanding, recall, listening, and speaking.

Follow complete A1, A2, and B1 learning paths, practise vocabulary in focused sets, and return exactly where you stopped. Noun articles are reinforced with visual memory cues, while natural Vivian pronunciation helps you hear each word clearly.

When you want a conversational session, Mia guides the lesson by voice. When you prefer to learn quietly, every chapter is also available in self-study mode.

Features:

- Guided A1, A2, and B1 learning paths
- Vocabulary sets with article and gender memory cues
- Natural German pronunciation
- Hands-free voice lessons with Mia
- Self-study mode for every chapter
- Progress that syncs across sessions
- Apple Watch vocabulary review

An internet connection is required for pronunciation, voice lessons, and account synchronization.

## Review notes

The app provides both self-study and voice-assisted German lessons. Microphone access is requested only when the learner starts a lesson with Mia. The microphone is used to transcribe the learner’s spoken German and continue the lesson. Self-study lessons work without microphone permission.
